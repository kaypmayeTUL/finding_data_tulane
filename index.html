🎯 Before you search

Three questions to ask before you search.

Skip these and you'll end up hip-deep in irrelevant datasets. If you can't answer them yet, start with a literature review — see what data other scholars in your area used, and where they got it.

Question 01

Who would have collected this?

  • Governments & agencies
  • International organizations
  • Data archives & scholars
  • Businesses & trade groups
Question 02

What kind of data do you need?

  • Topic / variables
  • Unit of analysis
  • Geographic unit
  • Time frame
Question 03

Why was it collected?

  • Research — deliberate study
  • Administrative — byproduct
  • Purpose shapes format & access

📚 Sources & purposes

Where data comes from — and why.

Two dimensions shape what a dataset looks like and how you'll access it: who publishes it, and why it was collected.

Who publishes the data

🏛️ Government

Usually free; policy-driven or administrative.

Ex.Census · BLS · CDC · LDEQ · data.un.org

🌐 International orgs

Free or subscription; cross-national policy focus.

Ex.UN · WHO · OECD · World Bank · MSF

📦 Data archives

Subscription; aggregates scholar-collected work.

Ex.ICPSR · Roper Center

💼 Business & trade

Proprietary; requires institutional subscription.

Ex.Market research · industry stats · financials
Why it was collected
Mode A · Research

Collected deliberately to answer a research question or inform policy. Structured for analysis from the start.

Ex.American Time Use Survey · General Social Survey
Mode B · Administrative

Byproduct of an organization operating — transactions, applications, filings, receipts.

Ex.SNAP recipients by parish · LA gaming revenue

🔎 Pathway 04 · Library Search

The Datasets filter in Library Search.

🔎
Library Search
search.library.tulane.edu
→

Library Search covers books, articles, media — and datasets. Apply the Datasets filter to surface data records alongside everything else. Good for a quick first sweep before diving into a subject-specific archive.

STEP 01

Open Library Search

Start at search.library.tulane.edu — the main library discovery layer.

STEP 02

Search your topic

Enter keywords like a normal catalog search — subject, region, time period.

STEP 03

Apply the filter

In "Refine my results," expand Resource Type and select Datasets.

STEP 04

Follow the links

Each result links out to its source — ICPSR, Sage Data, agency portals, and more.
